The desk

How a mix comes together.

A hands-on look at how a mix is built. Play a track, adjust the faders or mute a channel, and compare the raw recording with the finished mix.

Press play, then move a fader

Faders run from silence to +6 dB, with 0 dB marked in colour. Mute cuts a channel completely. Raw is the take as recorded. Mixed adds bus compression, a low-mid cut, high-end air and a short plate reverb.

Questions

Common questions.

Can I bring my own producer or instrumental?

Yes. Send stems or a stereo file before the session and we will have it loaded when you arrive. Your producer is welcome in the control room.

How long does mixing take?

Most songs come back within 48 hours with one round of revisions included. Rush turnaround is possible when the schedule allows.

Do I need to pay up front?

A 30% deposit holds your slot. The balance is due at the end of the session, before files are released.

What files do I get?

A WAV master and an MP3 of every song. Instrumentals and acapellas are available on request.

What is your cancellation policy?

Move or cancel up to 48 hours before your session at no charge. Inside 48 hours the deposit is kept, and it can be applied to a rescheduled session once.

Is there a minimum booking length?

Recording and vocal sessions start at two hours. Mixing and mastering are priced per song, with no minimum.

What if I arrive late?

We hold your slot for 20 minutes. After that the session is shortened to the time remaining, because the room is usually booked straight after.

How many revisions are included?

Every mix includes one round of revisions. Further rounds are charged at a reduced rate. Send notes in one message so changes can be made together.

Who owns the music?

You do. Recording, mixing and mastering work leaves the studio with all rights belonging to the artist. A release agreement only applies if you are signed.

How do I pay?

Card, bank transfer or mobile money. The deposit is requested when you confirm the booking, and a receipt is sent for every payment.

How do I get signed?

Book a session and bring your strongest material. We look for artists with a distinct sound and a clear direction, and we invite the ones we believe in to discuss a release agreement.
