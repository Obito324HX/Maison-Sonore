07Questions

Common questions.

Can I bring my own producer or instrumental?

Yes. Send stems or a stereo file before the session and we will have it loaded when you arrive. Your producer is welcome in the control room.

How long does mixing take?

Most songs come back within 48 hours with one round of revisions included. Rush turnaround is possible when the schedule allows.

What files do I get?

A WAV master and an MP3 of every song. Instrumentals and acapellas are available on request.

How do I pay?

Card, bank transfer or mobile money. The deposit is requested when you confirm the booking, and a receipt is sent for every payment.

How do I get signed?

Book a session and bring your strongest material. We look for artists with a distinct sound and a clear direction, and we invite the ones we believe in to discuss a release agreement.
